Our family story

Italian food. Family roots.
A place at the table.

From Little Italy
to Loch Raven.

The Pastore family’s story begins with importing Italian foods in Baltimore’s Little Italy. In the family account shared by owner Vince Pastore, Italian food and the family shop go hand in hand.

The retail store moved to Loch Raven in 1979. That family history is part of the delly you’ll find here today: sandwiches, prepared meals and Italian groceries to bring home.
